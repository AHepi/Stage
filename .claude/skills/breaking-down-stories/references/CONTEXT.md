# What the AI reads, the same for every story

These files are the kit's knowledge and formats. They do not change from one story to the next; a story's own files live in its project folder. Read them as rules to follow, and read only the parts a step file or a handout names.

Example: at step 8, the handout for a batch of shots holds the parts of cards 14, 10, 12, 13, 04 and 06 that step 8 names, the shot template from `templates/11 Scene.md` and the turn shot of `examples/01 The Catch - scene 10.md` (shot 150, "Not mint."); nothing else here is read for that unit.

| Folder | What it holds | Who reads it, and when |
|---|---|---|
| `cards/` | The craft cards `01` to `24`: reading a story, adapting prose, scenes and beats, dialogue, characters, voices, places and things, world and style, film rules, camera, light, staging, cutting and sound, shot design, action, glass and mirrors, screens, suspense, inner life, creatures and violence, AI pictures and video, grey previews, finishing, rights | Every step names the card parts it needs, for the depth and the scene's tags (`_config/schema/steps.json` lists them); at most `card_tokens_per_unit_max` card tokens a unit |
| `formats/` | `01 Record format` (the record grammar, and what you most often need), `02 Word list`, `03 Field guide` (every field in words), `04 Rule order`, `05 Quality rubric`, `06 Checks in words`, `07 Report and message formats`, `08 Commands` | Every unit that writes records follows `01` and `02`; every report follows `07`; step 10 and every check chat use `05` and `06`; a reply without code runs `06` part 1; `04` settles two rules that disagree; `08` on a code surface |
| `templates/` | The empty record files, one for each numbered project file that holds records, from `00 Start here` to `22 Rights and credits` | Step 0 starts `00` and `01` from them; every handout carries the template of each record type its unit writes; in a chat without code, the chat kit's `04 Templates and word list` |
| `examples/` | The gold example: The Catch, scene 10, finished at standard depth (`01`), and the whole-film records it cites (`02`) | Every handout quotes one example from it (left out when the scene is this very scene), and so does the self-test; `stage.py replay` checks it; `stage.py build-kit` makes the folder `02 Example - The Catch, scene 10` and the chat kit's example from it |
| `library/` | The research behind the cards, by code (`A1` to `D18`), each with a digest; `00 Resolved conflicts`, `01 What the codes mean`, `02 Errata` | Never read whole: `stage.py lib B1 R14` prints one rule with its errata. Without code, the cards are enough. The skill ZIP and the chat kit carry only the digests, the D files and the three notes |

Who may change these: the maintainers, never during a user's breakdown. A change here changes every breakdown made after it, so it is the place to fix a mistake that keeps coming back. `formats/03 Field guide.md` is made by `stage.py build-kit` from `_config/schema/schema.json`: change the schema, never the guide. After any change, run `stage.py build-kit` (the chat kit joins the cards, templates and formats) and the tests in `tests/`.
