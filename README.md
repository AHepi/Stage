# Stage

> **Scene 10, shot 150.** Close-up, camera still, about 15 seconds. Iona chews the leaf, stops, frowns, chews once more. "Not mint." We stay on her face while Saye answers from off the frame.
> **Why:** "Her face changes." The scene turns inside her mouth, so we don't cut away.

That is one shot from a plan Stage made for The Catch, a screenplay of 30 scenes. Stage turns your story (a screenplay, a novel, a short story or a play) into a scene-by-scene plan for making it as a film, including with AI picture and video tools. An AI app does the work in Claude, ChatGPT or Gemini; you answer a few choices, each with an answer ready, and a checker program checks every shot.

**Open [01 Read me first](01%20Start%20here/01%20Read%20me%20first.md).** It shows what you get, helps you pick your app and gives you the one message to type.

## In this folder

- **03 Kits to upload**: the kits for ChatGPT, Gemini and the Claude apps.
- **04 Project history**: how Stage was built and tested.
- **My stories** and **My breakdowns**: your stories, and the projects made from them.

Before you upload a story that is not published, turn off model training in your app; 01 Read me first says how.
