# 39 Test - The Catch - second full run

Log entry 39. The second complete run of the kit on all 30 scenes of The Catch, from "Break down my story." to the finished book, after the fixes of entries 32 to 38. It ran on 5 and 6 October 2026 in a separate test folder; your own breakdown of The Catch was not touched.

## In short

**The kit got all the way through, and much more cleanly than the first time.**
- The final check had no errors and 6 warnings; the first full run ended with 1 error and 126.
- All 30 scenes pass the kit's own scores; in the first run, 29 did.
- The scene work itself was nearly clean: 76 pieces of work for the 30 scenes, with 76 errors before repair, about one per piece.

## How the run was done

- **One helper at a time, all on Opus 5.5.** One planned the whole film, then one fresh helper per group of scenes (9 groups), then one finished the film. The first full run used Sonnet 5.5 for the scenes, so the comparison is rough.
- **The "user" was simulated:** "defaults" at every question, "next" at every group of shots, "no changes" at the end.
- **The helpers could use only the kit.** They could not read my notes or the tests, or change the kit. Each logged every problem: 230 reports in all.
- **It took about 12 hours and 20 minutes,** with no stop.

## What still went wrong

The helpers' 230 reports came down to 90 distinct problems. One helper then checked each against the kit:
- **63 were real kit faults or partly real.** All are listed in Project notes 40, with what was done about each.
- **22 were not kit faults:** 14 where the kit already said the right thing and the helper missed it, and 8 where the kit works as designed.
- **5 need a design decision.** They are in Project notes 41.

The most important real faults:
1. **A side choice deleted a state's other sides.** Answering which hand Iona's ring is on removed the sleeve and palm sides the state already had, and locked it. It cost three repair rounds and a made-up extra choice.
2. **Signs in the mirrored world could come out reading the right way round.** Wrong text pictures, and only half the reading time.
3. **Review answers were lost** when a scene's questions came in two batches.
4. **The scores could never start.** The full check called every missing score an error, while the step said scores come only after a full check with no error.
5. **Recordings shown on a screen counted as uses of a saved choice:** 10 false errors that blocked the scores.
6. **Handouts left out what a piece needed:**
   - people who never speak (22 scene and person pairs: the figure, the animal, the guard, the nurse, the technician);
   - secrets that involve two things;
   - the scene's look, at the scene design step;
   - every motif (none of 181 handouts showed one).
7. **All 46 video-prompt problems were faults of the prompt maker,** not of the records.

## The 6 warnings left at the end

- 1 false alarm: a person mid-crawl measured on a beat the cut skips.
- 3 real craft points, kept with reasons: two rhymes framed differently on purpose, and the cutting speeding up after scene 23 by design.
