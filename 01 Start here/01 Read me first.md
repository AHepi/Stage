# Read me first

## What you get

You give an AI app your story and type one message: **Break down my story.** Its first reply shows you one finished moment from your own story. For The Catch, a screenplay of 30 scenes, it looks like this:

```
Here is one finished moment from your story, so you can see what you'll get:
  Scene 10, shot 150. Close-up, camera still. Iona chews the leaf. She stops,
  frowns, chews once more, slowly. "Not mint." We stay on her face while Saye
  answers off screen. About 15 seconds.
  Why: "Her face changes." The scene turns inside her mouth, so we don't cut away.
```

By the end, every scene has a page with each shot on one line and its reasons. Three lines from scene 10's page:

```
shot 080, medium, 9 seconds: from far back along the table, the two women raise the
  hand nearest the camera, like a woman and her reflection; Eli small and deep in the middle
shot 150, close-up, 15 seconds, the turn: Iona chews, stops, chews once more; "Not mint.";
  we stay on her through Saye's answer
shot 190, wide, 9 seconds, the second turn: held from the high corner: "Look at Jude.
  I cannot finish that here."; Saye waits; Iona steps aside
```

The whole breakdown is a folder of numbered files you can read: the scene list, the characters, places and things, the film's rules, one page for each scene, a health check, the running time and cost, a readable book, a shot-list spreadsheet and captions. When you ask, it adds storyboards, grey previews (rough grey 3D versions of the hardest shots), prompts for AI video tools and a plan for the edit.

To see a finished scene now, open `15 The breakdown/The breakdown.html` in the folder **02 Example - The Catch, scene 10** in your web browser.

## Which app to use

| App | How well it works | What you need |
|---|---|---|
| Claude desktop with a connected folder | Best. The AI writes every file straight into your folder and checks each piece as it goes. | Claude desktop and a paid Claude plan |
| Claude Code | Best, and the only app that makes grey previews. | A paid Claude plan; Blender for grey previews |
| Claude website, with the skill | Good. The AI runs the checker itself; you download one save file at each stop. | Any Claude plan, the free one too |
| ChatGPT Plus | Good. The AI runs the checker in ChatGPT; you download one save file at each stop. | ChatGPT Plus or higher |
| Gemini | Works, with more saving by hand. | Gemini AI Pro or higher, and a free Claude account for the check |

Grey previews need Claude Code on your computer; everything else works in every app.

## What each app costs you

Prices are the makers' own, as of September 2026.

- **Claude desktop with a folder:** a paid Claude plan (Pro, about $20 a month); nothing to save by hand.
- **Claude Code:** the same plan; for grey previews, Blender too, which is free.
- **Claude website:** nothing on the free plan, but you reach its usage limit sooner; you download a save file at each stop.
- **ChatGPT Plus:** about $20 a month; download each save file at once, because ChatGPT's download links expire.
- **Gemini:** AI Pro, about $20 a month; you save each file yourself, about 80 for a 40-page script, and you still need a free Claude account to check the work.

**If you have neither Claude desktop nor ChatGPT Plus, use the Claude website's free plan with the skill.** It costs nothing, the AI runs the checker itself, and your work is kept in one save file at each stop. 02 Using Claude shows how.

## The first message

It is the same in every app:

```
Break down my story.
```

Attach your story to it. In Claude Code and in Claude desktop with a connected folder, put the story in the folder **My stories** instead. A plain text file, a Word file, a Fountain or Final Draft screenplay and an EPUB book all work. A PDF works only if it holds real text, not scanned pages; if the AI cannot read it, save the story as a Word or text file.

To carry on another day, in any app:

```
Continue my breakdown.
```

## How long it takes

- **The first reply** comes within minutes: one finished shot from your story and one question, whether the story is yours.
- **Your part:** you are asked to choose about four times: the scene list, the big choices (such as the film's style and its climax), the first group of shots, and the finished check. Each question has an answer ready; reply "defaults" to take them all.
- **The AI's part:** it works one group of scenes at a time (The Catch has 9). Claude desktop and Claude Code carry on by themselves between your choices; elsewhere one chat does one group. Plan on several sittings for a 40-page script.
- **Usage limits:** when you reach your plan's limit, the app pauses for a few hours. Nothing is lost: type "Continue my breakdown." when it resets.
- **Faster or deeper:** say "quick" for one line per shot, or "detailed" for more on every shot.

## Privacy

Before you upload a story that is not published, turn off model training in your app: "Help improve our AI models" in Claude, "Improve the model for everyone" in ChatGPT, "Keep Activity" in Gemini. Never use the free tier of Google AI Studio for an unpublished story. Stage puts no personal information in any file it writes. Guides 02, 03 and 04 show where each switch is.
